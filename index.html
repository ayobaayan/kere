Who We Are

A kinship of believers
called to serve

Kéré Alliance Foundation is a Christ-centered NGO incorporated under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. We are united in faith and committed to serving vulnerable and marginalized communities through compassionate, practical outreach.

Inspired by Matthew 25, we believe that serving others is a direct expression of our love for Christ. Every act of kindness — every meal shared, every shelter offered, every visit made — is a reflection of God's love in action.

What We Do

Programs that transform lives

Through five key programs, we meet practical needs and open doors for the Gospel to reach those who need it most.

🍲

Feed the Hungry

Combating food insecurity through community kitchens, food banks, and meal distribution.

  • Community kitchens & food banks
  • School meal partnerships
  • Food & Company (Road to Church)
👕

Threads of Hope

Restoring dignity through clothing and essential hygiene support for those in need.

  • Clothing drives & distribution
  • Hygiene kits for vulnerable families
🏠

Shelter & Housing

Providing safe haven for children, refugees, and displaced families across Nigeria.

  • Support for children's homes
  • Emergency shelter partnerships
  • Support for refugees & displaced families
🏥

Healthcare Outreach

Bringing healthcare and healing to underserved communities with compassion.

  • Mobile health clinics
  • Hospital visitation teams
⚖️

Prison & Reintegration

Bringing hope behind bars and equipping released individuals to rebuild their lives.

  • Prison visits & ministry
  • Rehabilitation programs
  • Job training for reintegration
🌍

Community Empowerment

Mobilizing volunteers, building partnerships, and running skills training programs.

  • Volunteer mobilization
  • Emergency relief response
  • Skills & empowerment training
