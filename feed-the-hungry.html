Our Projects

Where your gift makes a difference

Each project below addresses a specific need. Click Donate to support a project directly and make a real difference in someone's life.

🍲

Community Kitchens

We operate and support community kitchens that prepare and serve hot meals to hungry individuals and families in underserved neighbourhoods across Nigeria.

  • Hot meals served daily to families in need
  • Local volunteers and church partners mobilised
  • Safe, welcoming spaces that double as outreach points
  • Seasonal and festive feeding drives
📦

Food Banks & Meal Distribution

Our food banks collect, store, and distribute dry and fresh food supplies to families who would otherwise go without. We partner with local churches and NGOs to maximise reach.

  • Dry goods and food hampers for families
  • Partnership with churches and community centres
  • Emergency food relief during crisis periods
  • Regular distribution schedules in target communities
🏫

School Meal Partnerships

Children learn better when they are fed. We partner with schools in low-income communities to provide nutritious meals, reducing absenteeism and improving academic outcomes.

  • Daily school feeding programmes
  • Partnerships with public and mission schools
  • Nutritional education for students and parents
  • Support for boarding schools in rural areas
🛤️

Food & Company (Road to Church)

Food is a bridge to the Gospel. Through this initiative, we share meals with people on the margins and gently invite them into relationship with Christ and community.

  • Street feeding paired with Gospel sharing
  • Welcoming meals for church visitors and seekers
  • Discipleship follow-up for new contacts
  • Collaboration with local church congregations
"Every meal we serve is an act of worship — and an open door to the Kingdom."
— Feed the Hungry, Kéré Alliance Foundation
